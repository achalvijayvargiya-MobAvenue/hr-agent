Title: Manager - Finance (Taxation)

--- EXACT ZOHO DESCRIPTION ---
Key Responsibilities:  Preparation and filing of Income Tax returns, TDS returns, and other statutory returns.  Ensure compliance with provisions of the Income Tax Act, GST laws, and other applicable regulations.  Monthly review of TDS deductions, GST reconciliation's, and tax payments.  Handling notices, assessments, scrutiny proceedings, and correspondence with tax authorities.  Preparation of tax workings, advance tax computations, and tax provisioning.  Coordination with tax consultants and auditors for timely closure of assessments and audits.  Monitoring tax updates and assessing their impact on the business.  Support management reporting and MIS preparation.  Preparation of detailed cost sheet.  Reconciliation of vendors Skills Required  Strong knowledge of Income Tax, TDS, GST, and corporate taxation.  Good understanding of accounting standards and financial reporting.  Proficiency in MS Excel and ERP systems.  Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.  Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities.  Ability to work independently and manage multiple deadlines. Preferred Attributes  Detail-oriented with a compliance-focused approach.  Ability to handle assessments and tax litigations independently. Requirements Qualified CA 2+ years of relevant experience in direct and indirect taxation, financial reporting, and compliance. Exposure to ERP systems such as Zoho, Tally, or similar platforms will be preferred. Benefits  Opportunity to shape the people strategy of a fast-growing AdTech company.  Work closely with founders and senior leadership on strategic business initiatives.  Build and scale high-performing teams in a dynamic, innovation-driven environment.  Lead transformational HR initiatives with a significant organizational impact.
